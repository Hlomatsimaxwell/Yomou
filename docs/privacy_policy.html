Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-02

Yomou is a manga reader. It stores your library, reading progress, favorites, bookmarks and settings locally on your device. Yomou does not collect personal data, run analytics, or include advertising.

Network requests are made only to the manga sources you choose and to optional services you enable (cloud backup, update checks). Source requests use the source's own endpoints.

Optional cloud backup (Google Drive / WebDAV) uploads a backup of your library to a location you choose; Yomou never reads or transmits anything else. You can disable or delete these at any time.

You can export or erase all your data from Settings → Reset. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

Contact: see the project repository for support.
