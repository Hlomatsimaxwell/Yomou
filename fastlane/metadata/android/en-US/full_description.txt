Yomou is a manga reader inspired by Kotatsu-Redo, built for the long haul: multi-source support, reading progress, offline reading, and a clean Material 3 interface.

Features:
- Browse and read manga from many sources, with source presets and per-source settings
- Reading progress rings, mark-as-completed, and history
- Favorites with pinning
- Downloads for offline reading
- Suggestions / Explore feed for new chapters
- Statistics and backup/restore
- Incognito mode
- Theming and app icon switcher

Open source under Apache-2.0.
